# Why you dive

The developer's question: what do you seek out as you dive into planets?
"You need to seek out and obtain things and then cash them in."

- **Fuel.**
  Deuterium from ice, helium-3 from gas giants.
  Needed to keep moving.
- **Survey data.**
  The record of the Column is itself the product, sold to whoever wants to know what is down there.
  The developer especially liked surveying and selling data.
- **Deep materials.**
  Things that exist only at pressure, needed for the next hull grade.
- **The loop.**
  Go deeper, get materials, upgrade the hull, go deeper.
- **Mining is a by-product of travel.**
  Everything cut must become lining, propellant, heat sink, Feedstock or cargo, so "what do I do with this rock?" is asked every metre.
  Bulk ore hauling stays out; the vessel has little room for cargo.
- **The Column as a moving window.**
  You see a vein slide into the Bore and have only until it leaves the Tail to cut a piece out.
  Slowing down gives more time but costs heat and schedule.
