# Not yet designed

- Flight in air, space and gas giants beyond the three-step ascent.
- Spin gravity, given what the prototype showed.
- The vessel's actual dimensions.
- Warp.
- Ports, and who buys the data.
- Art style beyond the scan display.
- Pressure as an active mechanic beyond gating depth by hull grade.
- A helmet display: the crew's readings worn rather than read off a Station (developer's idea, second play test).
- A dive plan of legs (turns of chosen radius, straight runs) that Navigation projects and the Drive flies.
- What the Arm and the slides cost, and how they fail: the Stations' slide to the new floor and the Arm's run along the Bore are free for now, drawing no power and never failing.
- Phase as a third control of the Counter-wave.
- Coasting, and what the ring of Stations does in it: spec #71 asks that the Stations not move "in level and coast", but the simulation has no coasting state (the Head either cuts or stands still), so only level is built and tested; the ring's behaviour while coasting waits for a coasting state.
- Hazards met in a Glide (voids, water, magma): the Glide's only risk so far is where it leaves the crew, halted, untuned and unscanned.
- What a target can be, and what each is worth.
- A fibre line paid out in the glass of the bore, carrying light past the sealed tunnel; a later upgrade (developer's idea, 28 November play test). Real basis: fibre-guided drones and wire-guided torpedoes pay out tens of kilometres from a spool.
- Radiation, and whether it drives the window shutters (developer's idea, 28 November grill).
- The screen on which the crew reorders power priorities: the priorities are modelled, not shown.
