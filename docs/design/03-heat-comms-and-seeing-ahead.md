# Heat, comms and seeing ahead

## Heat

- Underground there is nowhere to radiate heat.
  The only sinks are the melt, the surrounding rock and the Column.
  In space you radiate.
  Heat is the central resource in every Medium.
- **The key dilemma.**
  Heat can go outside and hurt the vessel, or inside and hurt the Column.
  Cooking the Column may be the best decision for mobility while ruining much of what the dive is trying to acquire.
  Income and survival sit on the same dial.

## Comms

The developer's idea: treat the tunnel like fibre optics, or use sonar, switching comm modes depending on the material.

- **Open glass-lined tunnel:** light down the tunnel, high bandwidth.
- **Sealed in rock:** sound through the rock, a trickle.
- **Air or space:** radio or laser.
- **Bandwidth decides when you get paid.**
  Survey data sells only once uploaded.
  Sealed in, you carry unsold data home or dribble it out and choose what goes first.

## Seeing ahead

- The Column shows only what the vessel is already inside.
  Looking forward needs instruments: sound pulses for structure, gravity readings for density, heat readings for magma.
- The picture ahead is fuzzy and interpreted; the record behind is perfect.
  Hazards (voids, water, gas pockets, magma, faults) appear as ambiguous readings the navigator must judge.
  The Column confirms or refutes the guess seconds later.
- **Display.**
  An accumulating 3D point cloud at the Navigation Station, with Scanner Sombre as the visual reference.
  Points are sparse and coloured by confidence, sharpening with proximity or repeated pulses.
- **Quiet Ship.**
  For a clean scan the vessel halts, the Head idles and the Bore instruments shutter, because the vessel's own cutting noise drowns the echo.
  A pulse taken under way is noisy.
  The developer suggested a safety procedure that blinds other systems during a scan; this is that procedure with a physical reason.
- **No outside camera underground.**
  The only views are the Habitat, the instruments and the Bore ("but the Column will scroll by").
  This keeps the submarine feeling, and it means the planet underground never has to be built as 3D terrain, only described as which Medium is at each point.
  This is the largest single saving in the design.
