# Navigation's screens

- Navigation's point cloud (`ScanDisplay`, `view/scan_display.gd`) is three candidate views of one scan, awaiting the developer's pick (`docs/design/14-after-second-checkpoint-as-built.md`); the simulation holds which is shown (`scan_view()`, one of `scan_views()`, the forward scope when a dive starts).
  The forward scope is the vessel's own vertical plane with ahead up the screen, range rings about the Head and a plumb line to where down is (in a dive, onward along the compass heading is to the right, so a turn bends as it does on the moving map).
  Its range steps between a few fixed ranges (`SCOPE_RANGES_M`, `scope_shown()`) to keep the nearest Inferred Boundary ahead in view, changing only on a pulse; the heading line is a share of the range, so a Boundary 30 m ahead is drawn clear of the marker. The world-up view keeps its one scale.
  The plan and section pair is a top-down view beside a side-on one, ahead to the right in both and at one scale, nothing in 3D.
  The world-up 3D view has deeper always lower and goes round only with the compass heading.
  The debug key F4 (anywhere, `debug_next_scan_view()`) flips to the next, and the display's caption names the one shown, its number and the key.
  Surface control's survey points are drawn apart from the vessel's own in all three, small open crosses, faint in the legend's grey, never a filled square (`ScanDisplay.SURVEY_PX`, `SURVEY_ALPHA`), since they are one branch of `_draw_scan()`; `navigation.txt`'s "survey beside own" tiles show them.
  How the views are drawn, and the rules for changing them: `docs/conventions/scan.md`, "Drawing the scan".
- The Beam shows in every view as its cone (faint cyan fans out to `beam_reach()`) and the label `beam_label()` ("BEAM NARROW 30 deg") on the strip under the views; Navigation's status lines give it a line of its own (width, cone, reach, and "pulses as it goes" or "by hand only"), so the screen had six lines with the rock's.
  It has seven with the Glide's after it (`NAVIGATION_LINES`): the plot against what the scan shows, or the range left (`habitat.gd::_glide_plot_line()`).
  Aimed off the vessel's axis (#120) the cone is drawn about `beam_axis()`, a small hollow cyan diamond sits a fixed few pixels along the aim (`AIM_MARK_AT_PX`; on the Head when seen end-on), and the label and the Beam line add the aim in `ScanDisplay.aim_words()` ("AIM R45 U15"); dead ahead, none of these show; `navigation.txt`'s "Beam aimed" tiles show them.
- A target the Orders name shows in every view as a fuzzy cyan region, denser toward its middle, "TARGET A" beside it, narrowing as its points come in (`ScanDisplay._draw_targets()`, `region_outline()`; `targets.md`); `navigation.txt`'s "target before / after prospecting" tiles show it.
- A running Sweep shows in every view as a faint green band each side of its axis, in the Beam's two planes, from the Beam's edge out to where its spiral has reached, its leading edge strong (`_draw_sweep()`), its points joining the cloud a batch at a time.
  `sweep_label()`, on the strip's line over the Beam's, names its range and, while it runs, its share done and the time left ("SWEEP 200 m  45%  1:06 left"), else how long it takes and its key.
  The status lines are unchanged: the Sweep lives in the display.
- The plotted Glide is a broad cyan bar down the Course from the vessel with a bar across its end, the running one green to its end (`ScanDisplay._draw_glide()`, one call in `_draw_scan()`, so every view has it).
- The rock beyond each Inferred Boundary shows in every view as a band on its far side from the Head, hatched for harder rock and stippled for softer, in the points' colours, and surface control's names for the rocks it expects at the pane's right, marked as a guess until the Column shows them.
  The sixth status line (`ScanDisplay.rock_label()`) says it for the Boundary ahead; how, in `rock-estimate.md`.
- **The words on the scan display (#151): one layout in all three views.**
  Over the panes, a strip for the caption; under them, a strip (`ScanDisplay.TOOL_STRIP_PX`) with a line for each tool's state, the Sweep's over the Beam's (`tool_labels()`, `over_labels()`), so a tool's label keeps one place and never lies over the scan, a scale or another word; these shrink below the small font only as a narrow display needs (`FONT_SIZE_LEAST`).
  Inside a pane every word goes through `_pane_labels()` and `ScanLabels` (`view/scan_labels.gd`), placed in order: the pane's title, the scale's numbers (a ring's range, a depth, DOWN, N, "+100 m"; every fourth ring where every second would crowd), surface control's rock names (moved a line or two, or to the pane's left, to clear them), then the targets' names.
  Each goes at the first of its candidate places that is whole in the pane and clear of every word before it and of the vessel's and the aim's marks, else is left out, so a lesser word gives way and no two overlap; each sits on a faint backdrop so the points never hide it.
  A new word on the display goes through `ScanLabels.place()` with its candidates, never a bare `draw_string`; `label_boxes()` gives every box, and `scan_display_test.gd` checks them in crowded states, in every view, at the game's size and in a narrow display.
- **Marks told apart where the screen is seen small (#151):** the vessel's own echoes are filled squares and the survey's open crosses; harder rock beyond a Boundary is slanted strokes and softer clusters of dots, drawn over the points; all in the points' white and grey, so nothing reads as a caution.
- Navigation's moving map (`MovingMap`, `view/moving_map.gd`) is a 2D cross-section of the whole dive, redrawn a few times a second: across is ground distance along the unrolled track (`MovingMap.profile()` of `remembered_path()`, the simulation's perfect record of the path), down is depth, at one scale both ways.
  Its Boundaries are the Inferred Boundaries (`inferred_boundaries()`), never the layout: each patch drawn side-on between its corners, placed across by how far ahead of the Head along the heading they lie (`MovingMap.across()`), as sure as its `confidence`.
  The target depth is `target_depth()`, from the Orders (`Scenario.Goal.target_depth`, NAN for a goal with none).
- The projected path (`projected_path()`) is a solid cyan line on both of Navigation's displays, beside the Course's dashes: among the points in every candidate view, on the map unrolled onward from the Head (`MovingMap.ahead()`), and the map's scale makes room for it.
