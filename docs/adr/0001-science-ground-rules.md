# Science ground rules: three impossible things, real relationships, tuned numbers

The game is hard science fiction, but its destinations (a gas giant's core, other planets within a play session) are out of reach of real materials and real travel times. We allow exactly three impossible things: compact fusion, the hull material, and warp travel between planets. Everything else must keep its physical relationships true (more speed needs more power, more power makes more heat, deeper means more pressure), while the constants in those relationships are set for play rather than taken from reality.

## Considered Options

- **Real materials and real numbers only**: rejected because it rules out deep interiors and makes a trip through the Moon take about 40 days of cutting.
- **Soft science fiction**: rejected because the simulated systems are the game, and they only feel earned if their trade-offs are real.

## Consequences

A new mechanic that needs a fourth impossible thing should be reworked or argued for by amending this record, not added quietly.
