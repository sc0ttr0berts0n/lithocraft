# The Habitat's room: its height from the floor and from the Arm, as ticket #68 named, and the ring of
# Stations at each end and mid-slide, as ticket #69 named.
# From the repo root: tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/habitat.txt
# The ring tiles look into the Bore from the outer wall, level with the ring, the Head end down: the Head
# end floor is under the ring at the Head end, the Tail end cap over it at the Tail end, neither mid-slide.
# The slide is a climb's: from 800 m down at full power, steering hard for straight up, the ring sets off
# as the climb passes 45 degrees, near dive time 2498 s, and takes 6 s.
# Debug commands: debug_skip in the last two tiles (the climb is forty minutes into the dive).

from the floor, in a dive: --look crew --time 5 --tilt 55 --do "0 set_power 0.5"
from the Arm, at the Drive: --look Drive --time 5 --turn 180 --tilt 30 --do "0 set_power 0.5"

ring at the Head end, in a dive: --look bore --time 5 --do "0 set_power 0.5"
ring mid-slide, in a climb: --look bore --time 2501 --do "0 start_below 800" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_autopilot reactor true" --do "0 set_course 0 -90" --do "1 debug_skip 2400"
ring at the Tail end, in a climb: --look bore --time 2506 --do "0 start_below 800" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_autopilot reactor true" --do "0 set_course 0 -90" --do "1 debug_skip 2400"
