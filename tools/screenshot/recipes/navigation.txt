# Navigation's screen: each candidate scan view (ScanDisplay) diving and mid-turn, as ticket #67 named,
# then its Course lines with the Drive following the Course and off it, as ticket #65 named,
# then the Beam's three widths and its own pulsing, as ticket #117 named.
# Every scan view shows the Beam's cone (faint cyan fans to its reach) and names its width in the caption;
# the forward scope's range steps in to keep the nearest Inferred Boundary ahead in view (the diving tiles: 60 m).
# From the repo root: tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/navigation.txt
# Diving: the Shift drill, straight down 34 m above the sandstone, three pulses sent.
# Mid-turn: the First dive at full power, steering hard from straight down toward level on heading 090,
# about 20 deg into the turn, the sandstone some 15 m below, three pulses sent.
# Debug commands: debug_next_scan_view, once or twice, for the second and third candidate view
# (the crew has no control for it; its key is the debug key F4).

forward scope, diving: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
forward scope, mid-turn: --look Navigation --time 85 --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_course 90 0" --do "75 pulse" --do "80 pulse" --do "84 pulse"

plan and section, diving: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
plan and section, mid-turn: --look Navigation --time 85 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_course 90 0" --do "75 pulse" --do "80 pulse" --do "84 pulse"

world-up 3D, diving: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
world-up 3D, mid-turn: --look Navigation --time 85 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_course 90 0" --do "75 pulse" --do "80 pulse" --do "84 pulse"

# The same two dives as drive.txt's first two tiles; the Drive is attended for the moment of its nudges.
Drive follows the Course: --look Navigation --time 30 --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "0 set_course 90 60"
Drive off the Course, 30 deg up: --look Navigation --time 30 --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "0 set_course 90 60" --do "20 set_attended drive true" --do "20 nudge_pitch -1" --do "20 nudge_pitch -1"

# The Beam's widths, diving as above: wide and short against narrow and long, the cone drawn at its width and
# Navigation's Beam line naming it, its angle and reach. Wide in the forward scope, narrow in all three views.
forward scope, wide Beam: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 set_beam_width wide" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
forward scope, narrow Beam: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 set_beam_width narrow" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
plan and section, narrow Beam: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_beam_width narrow" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"
world-up 3D, narrow Beam: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Navigation --time 10 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_beam_width narrow" --do "4 pulse" --do "8 pulse"

# The Beam pulsing by itself: the First dive under way at full power with the switch on and no key pressed;
# the Beam line says it pulses as it goes and the cloud grows as the vessel covers ground.
Beam pulsing by itself, under way: --look Navigation --time 60 --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_beam_pulsing true"

# Surface control's survey beside the vessel's own points (ticket #118): the First dive from the surface,
# Quiet Ship at 20 s some 40 m down and one clean pulse at 35 s. Under the pulse's cone the vessel's own
# filled squares have replaced the survey's; round them, and at the granite below, the survey's faint
# hollow grey squares and its faint patches remain. Every other tile above starts surveyed too.
forward scope, survey beside own: --look Navigation --time 40 --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "20 set_quiet_ship true" --do "35 pulse"
plan and section, survey beside own: --look Navigation --time 40 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "20 set_quiet_ship true" --do "35 pulse"
world-up 3D, survey beside own: --look Navigation --time 40 --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 debug_next_scan_view" --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "20 set_quiet_ship true" --do "35 pulse"
