# then the Beam aimed, as ticket #120 named.
# Every scan view shows the Beam's cone (faint cyan fans to its reach) and names its width in the caption;
# the forward scope's range steps in to keep the nearest Inferred Boundary ahead in view (the diving tiles: 60 m).
# From the repo root: tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/navigation.txt
# Diving: the Shift drill, straight down 34 m above the sandstone, three pulses sent.
# Mid-turn: the First dive at full power, steering hard from straight down toward level on heading 090,
# about 20 deg into the turn, the sandstone some 15 m below, three pulses sent.
# Debug commands: debug_next_scan_view, once or twice, for the second and third candidate view
# (the crew has no control for it; its key is the debug key F4).

forward scope, mid-turn: --look Navigation --time 85 --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_course 90 0" --do "75 pulse" --do "80 pulse" --do "84 pulse"
