# The Reactor's screen (PlasmaDisplay) in the states ticket #63 named.
# From the repo root: tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/reactor.txt
# The Shift drill starts 40 m above the sandstone with one pulse sent under way.
# Debug commands: debug_wear_head in "worn and hot" (a Head worn half through is many Boundaries away).

# ACTIVE cancels the sand's Wobble: the Residual flat inside the Band, no lamp lit, no scan yet.
steady in the sand: --look Reactor --time 20 --do "0 set_power 0.5"

# One pulse under way, STANDBY tuned to the guess: GUESS SURE is low, the predicted Residual ripples
# and its verdict is IN BAND IF THE SCAN IS RIGHT.
Boundary ahead, poor scan: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Reactor --time 20 --do "2 tune_frequency 1.29" --do "2 tune_amplitude 0.65"

# Four more pulses under Quiet Ship, then under way again: GUESS SURE is high, the predicted Residual near flat.
Boundary ahead, Quiet Ship scan: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Reactor --time 20 --do "0 set_quiet_ship true" --do "1 pulse" --do "2 pulse" --do "3 pulse" --do "4 pulse" --do "6 set_quiet_ship false" --do "6 tune_frequency 1.27" --do "6 tune_amplitude 0.63"

# The Head crosses near 67 s with STANDBY prepared and no swap made: BOUNDARY CROSSED and the swap's key,
# ACTIVE's Residual amber outside the Band, OUT OF BAND lit.
just past a Boundary, before the swap: --scenario "Shift drill" --look Reactor --time 70 --do "2 tune_frequency 1.29" --do "2 tune_amplitude 0.65"

# Full power with all heat outward and a Head worn half through: the Band about half its widest.
# ACTIVE's amplitude is a little off for the last 2 s, a Residual a fresh, cool Head's Band would hold,
# so HEAD WORN, HOT and OUT OF BAND are all lit.
worn and hot: --look Reactor --time 60 --do "0 set_power 1" --do "1 debug_wear_head 0.5" --do "58 select_channel active" --do "58 tune_amplitude 0.63"

# ACTIVE tuned off the Wobble in frequency alone: the Residual beats, swelling and fading.
wrong frequency: --look Reactor --time 20 --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "14 select_channel active" --do "14 tune_frequency 1.0"

# ACTIVE tuned off the Wobble in amplitude alone: the Residual is a wave of one size.
wrong amplitude: --look Reactor --time 20 --do "0 set_power 0.5" --do "14 select_channel active" --do "14 tune_amplitude 0.8"

# The Glide (#123), on the First dive from the surface: started at 1 s, 2 s in. No Wobble on the plasma:
# ACTIVE's lane a flat Wobble and no Residual; the first line "Head GLIDE: no Wobble"; Drv far past full.
gliding: --look Reactor --time 3 --do "1 plot_glide 300" --do "1 start_glide"

# A Glide of 150 m, over by 6 s, the Head at idle: "Head AT IDLE: tune STANDBY here, [R] spins up"; STANDBY's
# lane reads against the rock round the vessel.
out at idle: --look Reactor --time 8 --do "1 plot_glide 150" --do "1 start_glide"
