# The Checklist panel (Tab, ChecklistPanel), opened over the look with --checklist, which does not read the
# orders: its colours (#92), the "Turn and climb" phase's three steps (#73) and surface control's warning
# that the turn for home deepens (#89), at the panel's foot.
# From the repo root: tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/checklist.txt
# Colours are the Station colour language's only: done green, the next step and its key white and bold with
# its Station in cyan, the other steps white with their Station in legend grey, "coming down the link" grey.
# Debug commands: debug_skip in the last tile (the climb is minutes into the turn).

# Startup at the surface, the orders read and the Reactor's autopilot engaged: two steps done in green,
# the next (Thermal's autopilot) bold with "Thermal" in cyan, the rest white with their Stations grey.
startup, two steps done: --look Navigation --time 5 --checklist --do "0 read_orders" --do "1 set_autopilot reactor true"

# In the sandstone above the granite, under way at full power, a climb plotted at 1 s: the Lithocraft is
# still diving as it begins to come round, and the warning "Turning for home: you will deepen through the
# turn." has come up the open tunnel to the foot of the panel.
warned of the turn, still diving: --look Drive --time 30 --checklist --do "0 start_below 300" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_autopilot reactor true" --do "1 set_course 0 -90"

# The same dive come round to the climb: "Turn and climb" running with its three steps, the climb plotted,
# the Reactor's autopilot for the climb and the turn for home, all done as the phase began.
turn and climb, its three steps: --look Drive --time 600 --checklist --do "0 start_below 300" --do "0 set_power 1" --do "0 set_steering_effort 1" --do "0 set_autopilot thermal true" --do "0 set_autopilot reactor true" --do "1 set_course 0 -90" --do "2 debug_skip 500"
