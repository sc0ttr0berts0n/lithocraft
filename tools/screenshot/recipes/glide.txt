# The Glide seen (ticket #127): the Bore carries a river of glowing melt in place of the Column, its light
# floods the Habitat, and the tunnel it leaves is drawn rough. From the repo root:
# tools/screenshot/shot.sh --sheet tools/screenshot/recipes/glide.txt
# All on the First dive from the surface, which starts surveyed, so a Glide can be plotted at once.
# No debug commands.

# Before: the Crawl at half power, the Column sliding up the Bore, the room in its own white light.
bore before, crawling: --look bore --time 5 --do "0 set_power 0.5"

# A Glide of 400 m started at 1 s, 1 s into its 3 s spin-up: the whole Bore is melt, orange and lumpy, the
# room warming.
bore spinning up: --look bore --time 2 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide"

# 4 s in, at full thrust: the melt streams up the Bore, yellow-white streaks over orange, blooming; the room
# lit orange by it.
bore gliding, diving: --look bore --time 5 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide"

# No climbing tile yet: on the First dive's layout a climb's line leaves the surveyed rock before the Head
# is within a degree of an upward Course, so no Glide up can be plotted (#128 adds the Glide home and its
# tile). The melt always runs toward the Tail end, which is below the crew in a climb.

# Dropped out by the crew at 4 s, 1 s into the 2 s spin-down: the melt dimmer and slower, back to lumps.
bore dropping out: --look bore --time 5 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide" --do "4 drop_out_of_glide"

# A Glide of 150 m, over by 6 s, at 12 s: halted at idle, the melt in the Bore stopped and crusting dark red,
# glowing along its cracks; the room's light back to white.
bore at idle: --look bore --time 12 --do "1 plot_glide 150" --do "1 start_glide"

# The crew's own place in a Glide: the Habitat lit orange through the glass.
crew, gliding: --look crew --time 5 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide"
crew, looking up the Bore, gliding: --look crew --time 5 --tilt 55 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide"

# The Bore Station in a Glide: "No Column in the Bore: melted by the Glide" in amber.
Bore Station, gliding: --look "Bore Station" --time 5 --do "1 plot_glide 400" --do "1 start_glide"

# After the Glide, crawled on: the tunnel panel's strip behind the Tail names the rough wall, and
# Navigation's moving map draws the glided stretch of the path behind as rough.
Bore Station, after a Glide: --look "Bore Station" --time 40 --do "1 plot_glide 150" --do "1 start_glide" --do "9 shift" --do "9 set_power 1"
Navigation, after a Glide: --look Navigation --time 40 --do "1 plot_glide 150" --do "1 start_glide" --do "9 shift" --do "9 set_power 1"
